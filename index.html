Education

Recognize manipulation patterns online

How grooming begins

Predators often start by building trust through attention, praise, emotional support, or gifts to create a false sense of safety.

Warning signs to watch for

Requests for secrecy, pressure to move to private channels, and attempts to isolate a young person are common red flags.

Protecting your accounts

Use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and avoid sharing personal details with strangers online.

What to do if you suspect grooming

Document the behaviour, avoid direct confrontation, and report it to a trusted adult, moderators, or the relevant authorities.

Support

If you're struggling

If you or someone you know is feeling overwhelmed, unsafe, or emotionally distressed, you do not have to face it alone. Reaching out to a trained professional or someone you trust can make a real difference.

  • • Talk to a trusted friend, family member, teacher, or mentor.
  • • Reach out to a licensed mental health professional.
  • • Contact a local crisis hotline or emergency service in your area.
  • • If you feel in immediate danger, seek emergency help right away.
